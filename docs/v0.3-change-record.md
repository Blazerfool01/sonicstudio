# Genre Mixer v0.3 — Change Record

Date: 2026-10-04

## Changed

- Added `src/data/compatibility.json`: each genre now records an approach, arrangement layer, and role force for all seven musical dimensions.
- Added `src/data/oppositions.json`: explicit opposing approaches, so a close mix and a wide reverberant mix can register as a dispute even when their force values match.
- Added the pure `src/lib/compatibility.ts` analyzer. It classifies every dimension as **reinforcing**, **complementary**, **contrasting**, or **conflicting**. It uses the existing Sound DNA lead, the selected weights, musical layer, and the curated approach data to explain support, competition, control, and a resolution for contrasts or conflicts.
- Added the “How the sounds meet” section below the existing mixer and Sound DNA. The two-genre controls and v0.2 merge output were not changed. No overall score was added.

## Verification

- `npm test`: 12 passing tests, including all 66 pairs at 20/50/80 weights, all four classifications, explicit oppositions, the four requested review cases, and unchanged Sound DNA.
- `npm run build`: TypeScript and Vite production build pass.
- Running browser: the compatibility section rendered; Dark R&B/Hardwave changed from contrasting at 80/20 to conflicting at 50/50 and back to a Hardwave-led contrast at 20/80. Ambient/Drum & Bass and Synthwave/Hardwave displayed the classifications above.

The classifications are curated musical guidance. Role force measures the importance of a dimension within a genre profile; it is not a measured acoustic property or an overall compatibility score.
